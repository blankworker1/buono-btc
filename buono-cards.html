BUONO gift cards

Square cards on A4, printed on both sides. Paste the gift links from your wallet, then save the sheets as a PDF.

  1. In the print window choose Save as PDF, paper size A4, margins None, scale 100%, and switch Background graphics on.
  2. Print the PDF double-sided at actual size, in colour.
  3. Cut along the marks in the margins. Each card is 63 mm square.
  4. Cover each QR code with a peel-off sticker before handing a card over.

Each gift link is the same as cash. This page works entirely on your device and sends the links nowhere. Close it when you have finished, and delete the PDF once the cards are printed.
